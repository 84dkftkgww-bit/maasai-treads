About Us

Style that makes a difference.

Every product tells a story of resourcefulness, resilience and the power of reimagining what is possible.

Established in 2005, Maasai Treads is a Kenyan lifestyle brand transforming discarded materials into stylish, durable and functional products. We breathe new life into old car tyres, leather, denim, hemp and kikoy, creating distinctive footwear and accessories that combine creativity, craftsmanship and sustainability.

Our collections celebrate African-inspired design while embracing a more responsible approach to fashion and everyday living. From our signature tyre-soled sandals to thoughtfully crafted lifestyle products, every piece reflects our belief that materials destined for waste can have a new purpose.

Beyond our products, we are passionate about supporting local craftsmanship, creating livelihood opportunities and promoting environmental conservation. By working with skilled Kenyan artisans and championing the reuse of materials, we aim to create lasting value for people and the planet.

At Maasai Treads, we believe that style can make a difference. Every product tells a story of resourcefulness, resilience and the power of reimagining what is possible.

When you choose Maasai Treads, you become part of the movement to create a more sustainable future, one step at a time.
